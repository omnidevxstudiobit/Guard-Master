The guide

What Clear View security fencing actually is

The mesh that has no handholds

Clear View fencing — known in the trade as 358 mesh — is a welded steel mesh with apertures of 3" × 0.5" (76 × 12.7 mm). The opening is too narrow for a finger-grip or a boot tip, and too tight for the jaws of a bolt cutter to pass through. That geometry is the whole product: an intruder facing a Clear View panel has nothing to climb and nothing to cut, while anyone standing behind it keeps an almost uninterrupted view of the other side. It's why the same system guards mine perimeters, rail corridors, solar farms and family homes.

One system, engineered together

A Guard Master fence line is panels, posts, fixings and toppings designed as one system. Panels come 10 ft (3 m) wide in heights from 6 to 10 ft (1.8 – 3.0 m), in two wire gauges and three security grades. Posts are matched to panel height — a 6 ft panel takes an 8 ft post, with the balance set below grade — and anti-tamper spider clamps hold panel to post at every course. Solar post caps light the run after dark with no trenching or cabling.

Four finishes, honestly ranked

Plain galvanized is the working finish — bright zinc, the lowest price that still lasts outdoors. Hot-dip galvanizing carries a heavier zinc coat for coastal and industrial air. Powder coating adds color over galvanizing, usually matte black, which visually disappears against planting. Plascoat, a thick thermoplastic coat, is the premium system and the only finish we sell with a 10-year warranty.

Manufacturer, not reseller

Guard Master is an American company that manufactures in its own factory in Benoni South, South Africa — every panel welded at every crossing, then shipped across the continental United States and worldwide from the source. Owning the factory is the reason every product on this site carries a published price: there is no importer in the middle to hide one.

Before you order

What is Clear View 358 mesh?

A welded steel mesh with a 76 × 12.7 mm aperture — too tight for a toehold and too narrow for bolt-cutter jaws. Panels come 3 m wide, welded at every crossing.

Which aperture should I choose?

76 × 12.7 mm is high security, 76 × 25 mm medium, 76 × 50 mm standard. For a home boundary, the lighter Vista range covers 1.2 – 2.0 m heights at a lower price.

Which finish lasts longest?

All four are made for the outdoors. Plascoat is the only one carrying the 10-year warranty, and hot-dip galvanising is the usual choice for coastal air.

Do you make gates to match?

Yes — sliding gates from 3.0 to 6.0 m wide with the same mesh infill, so the boundary reads as one continuous line.

Do prices include VAT?

Yes. Every price on this site includes 15% VAT.

Can I collect my order?

Yes — collection is free from the factory at 10 Burnley Street, Benoni South, Gauteng.

Do you deliver?

Yes, nationwide — delivery is arranged per order when you check out.

Do you install?

We supply and install, or supply only — your call. Panels bolt between capped posts every 3 metres, so a competent contractor can also fit them.
