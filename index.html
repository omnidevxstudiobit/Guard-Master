Before you order

What is Clear View 358 mesh?

A welded steel mesh with a 76 × 12.7 mm aperture — too tight for a toehold and too narrow for bolt-cutter jaws. Panels come 3 m wide, welded at every crossing.

Which aperture should I choose?

76 × 12.7 mm is high security, 76 × 25 mm medium, 76 × 50 mm standard. For a home boundary, the lighter Vista range covers 1.2 – 2.0 m heights at a lower price.

Which finish lasts longest?

All four are made for the outdoors. Plascoat is the only one carrying the 10-year warranty, and hot-dip galvanising is the usual choice for coastal air.

Do you make gates to match?

Yes — sliding gates from 3.0 to 6.0 m wide with the same mesh infill, so the boundary reads as one continuous line.

Do prices include VAT?

Yes. Every price on this site includes 15% VAT.

Can I collect my order?

Yes — collection is free from the factory at 10 Burnley Street, Benoni South, Gauteng.

Do you deliver?

Yes, nationwide — delivery is arranged per order when you check out.

Do you install?

We supply and install, or supply only — your call. Panels bolt between capped posts every 3 metres, so a competent contractor can also fit them.
