Warranty

Products are manufactured in our own factory in Benoni South and are covered against manufacturing defects. The Plascoat finish carries a 10-year warranty — it is the only finish sold with one. Warranty terms for other finishes and for hardware are confirmed on your quotation or invoice.

Returns

Standard stock items in unused, original condition can be returned by arrangement with sales. Made-to-order items — including gates built to your opening width and panels cut or coated to spec — are manufactured for you and can't be returned unless faulty. Contact sales before returning anything so we can book it in.

Delivery & collection

Collection is free from the factory at 10 Burnley Street, Benoni South, Gauteng — Mon–Thu 07:30–16:30, Fri 07:30–13:30. Delivery is available nationwide and is quoted and arranged per order when you check out.

Privacy

The details you enter at checkout are used to process and deliver your order and for nothing else. We don't sell or share your information. To have your details removed, email info@fencing-supplier.com.
